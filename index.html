Experience

Independent Researcher and Developer

Machine learning, mechanistic interpretability & software

Pre-registered and ran a study of cross-layer dynamics in transformer factual recall across six language models from three families, entirely on consumer hardware. The paper, the frozen protocol and all code are public. Also ran a grokking (delayed generalisation) study on gesture-recognition networks trained on electromyography data.

Alongside the research, designed and shipped software end to end: edh-deck-diff, a web app in public beta that compares cEDH decks with the latest decks that did well at tournaments; funding-extract, a rules → classifier → LLM cascade for web-scale extraction, with a hard grounding gate and evals; a local RAG pipeline over Slovenian documents; and a telco churn model with SHAP explanations.
